How to read this guide FaceTrack has three portals. Students register themselves online (section 4). The administrator checks each student's identity in person and captures their face (section 5). Lecturers take attendance with the camera (section 6). Section 7 joins them into end-to-end journeys. If you are about to demonstrate the system, go straight to the checklist in section 10.

1. What FaceTrack does

Attendance that is taken by the camera, not by a register passed around the hall.

In most lecture halls attendance is still taken on paper. It eats lecture time, the sheets get lost, and students sign for friends who are not in the room. FaceTrack replaces that with a camera at the front of the class: a student looks at it, is recognised from their own face, and is marked present in under a second.

How the parts fit together

INPUT
Camera
The lecturer's webcam captures the room
→
PROCESSING
Browser
Corrects lighting, finds each face, turns it into 128 numbers
→
MATCHING
Server
Compares against verified students on that course
→
OUTPUT
Register
Attendance saved; reports for staff and students
Camera footage never leaves the computer The browser converts each face into a list of 128 numbers — a "descriptor" — and only those numbers are sent to the server. No photograph of the classroom is ever uploaded, and a descriptor cannot be turned back into a picture of a face.

What it gives you

CapabilityWhat it means in practice
Self-service registrationStudents register online from anywhere and pick their own courses. Every field is chosen from a list, so records are consistent and the admin office types almost nothing.
Verified identitiesA face is enrolled only in person, after the administrator checks the student against their JAMB result or admission letter. One face can never be enrolled under two matric numbers.
Fast, automatic captureEnrolment takes three face samples by itself as soon as a face is in view — typically in under two seconds — and corrects poor lighting automatically.
No proxy attendanceA student must present their own face. Naming somebody is not enough — matching happens on the server, against templates the browser never sees.
No double entriesA student who stays in front of the camera for ten minutes still produces exactly one record.
Late tracking & locked registersAnyone recognised after the cut-off is marked late; closing a session freezes the register.
Reports & eligibilityAttendance percentage per student against the course requirement (75% by default) — for staff as a register, and for each student on their own dashboard.

2. Before you start

Three things to check before your first lecture.

2.1  What you need

RequirementDetail
BrowserGoogle Chrome or Microsoft Edge (recommended), or Safari, kept up to date.
CameraAny built-in laptop webcam or USB camera. Needed only on the admin office PC (face enrolment) and the lecturer's PC (attendance). Students need no camera.
InternetNeeded to reach the site and save records. Face detection itself runs on the local machine.
Hardware accelerationLeave it switched on in the browser (Chrome: Settings → System → Use graphics acceleration when available). Without it capture still works, but slower.

2.2  Preparing the camera engine — open the page early

The first time a camera screen is opened in a browser, about 12 MB of face-recognition models download and the engine prepares itself. The camera button reads Preparing… and the panel shows what is happening (Loading face detector…, Preparing the camera engine (first time only)…). This usually takes 5–30 seconds and happens once per browser session: every capture after it is fast.

Open the camera page a minute before you need it Administrator: open a student's Verify & enrol page while the student is still walking to your desk. Lecturer: open the live capture screen while students settle. When the button changes to Start face capture / Start capture, the engine is ready.

Under the camera, a line reports the engine's mode. Face engine: GPU-accelerated is the normal, fast mode. CPU mode — capture will be slower means the browser's hardware acceleration is off; capture still works (about 7 seconds for an enrolment instead of under 2), and switching acceleration back on restores full speed.

3. Portals, accounts & passwords

Three kinds of user, each with their own portal and their own job.

3.1  The three portals

Student portal
Registers online from anywhere: personal and academic details, passport photograph, password. Registers their courses and follows their own attendance and exam eligibility. Cannot enrol a face.
Administrator portal
The department officer. Verifies each student in person against their JAMB result or admission letter and captures their face. Corrects records, creates courses, assigns lecturers, manages staff, monitors lectures.
Lecturer portal
Sees only the courses assigned to them. Starts a lecture session, takes attendance with the camera, marks stragglers manually, closes the register, reads the reports.
Why can students not capture their own face? Impersonation. If a face could be enrolled from home, a student could enrol a friend's face under their own matric number and send the friend to every lecture. So registration is self-service, but the face is captured only in person by the administrator, after an identity check — and the system refuses any face that is already enrolled under another matric number. Every rule is enforced on the server, not just by hiding buttons.

3.2  The sign-in screen

Everyone /login
  1. Open https://attendace-system-david.vercel.app/. You are taken to the sign-in screen.
  2. Choose your portal with the tabs: Student (the default), Lecturer or Administrator.
  3. Students enter their matric number; staff enter their email address. Then the password.
  4. Press Sign in. Each portal opens its own dashboard. New students use Create your account under the form.

4. The student portal

Registering from anywhere, and following your own attendance.

Sidebar: Dashboard · Course registration · My attendance Sign in on the Student tab with your matric number.

4.1  Registering online

Student /register

Open the site, choose the Student tab and click Create your account. The form has three parts, and almost every answer is picked from a list.

  1. Personal details — full name (surname first), gender (list), phone number and email address.
  2. Academic details — choose your faculty; the department list then shows only that faculty's departments. Choose your level and year of entry.
  3. Matric number — the first parts are filled in for you from your department code and year of entry (for example CSC/2022/). Type only the final digits; the full number is shown underneath.
  4. Type your JAMB registration number exactly as printed on your result slip.
  5. Upload a passport photograph — a clear, recent photo of your face. The administrator compares it with you in person.
  6. Choose a password, confirm it, and press Create my account. You are signed in straight away.
Your face is not captured online That happens only at the admin office (section 4.4). Registration is refused if the matric number or JAMB number is already registered.

4.2  Student dashboard

Student /dashboard

A three-step progress tracker shows what is done and what is next, beside your record and passport photo. Until your face is enrolled, an amber banner reminds you to visit the admin office.

4.3  Course registration

Student /my-courses
  1. Open Course registration. Courses on offer is already filtered to your department and level; change the Department, Level or Semester dropdowns to see others.
  2. Press Register beside each course you are taking. It moves to My registered courses, with your total units.
  3. Drop removes a course — allowed only until attendance has been recorded for you on it.

4.4  Visiting the admin office

Take your JAMB result slip or admission letter (or school ID card) to the admin office. The administrator compares you with the photograph on the document and with your uploaded passport, then captures your face with the camera — it takes under a minute. After that your dashboard shows Verified and the classroom camera can mark you present.

4.5  Attendance & exam eligibility

Student /dashboard · /my-attendance

Once verified, the dashboard lists each registered course with classes held, classes attended (and how many late), your percentage, and whether you meet the requirement to sit the exam. My attendance lists every lecture you were marked in, filterable by course.

5. The administrator portal

Verifying students, capturing faces, and keeping records correct.

Sidebar: Dashboard · Students · Courses · Staff accounts · Session monitor · Attendance reports Sign in on the Administrator tab.

5.1  Administrator dashboard

Admin /dashboard

A department-wide overview: how many students are awaiting verification, courses without a lecturer, lecturer accounts, attendance marked today and classes in progress. Verify students (top right) shows how many are waiting.

5.2  Students & the verification queue

Admin /students

Students appear here as soon as they register online. The page opens on Awaiting verification; the other tabs show Face enrolled and All students. Filter with the search box and the Department and Level dropdowns. Press Verify & enrol when a student arrives.

5.3  Verify & enrol — the in-person step

Admin /students/[id]

This is the most important screen in the system. It ties a face to a verified identity.

  1. Open the page early — ideally while the student is walking to your desk. The camera panel shows the engine preparing (Figure 11); wait for Start face capture.
  2. Check the identity. Compare the student with their uploaded passport photo (shown on the page) and the photograph on their document. Choose the document from Document checked (JAMB result slip, JAMB admission letter, school admission letter or student ID card); the JAMB number is pre-filled. Tick the confirmation box. The camera stays locked until you do (Figure 12).
  3. Press Start face capture and ask the student to look at the camera. Nothing else to press: three samples are taken automatically as soon as a face is in view, while on-screen prompts suggest a slight turn left and right (Figure 13).
  4. When the panel turns green — Face captured in 1.4 s — press Verify student & save face. Use Retake if you are not happy.
Lighting is corrected automatically Each camera frame's brightness is measured and a dim or washed-out image is corrected before detection; a note appears under the camera when this happens. Capture still works best with light on the student's face rather than behind them.

5.4  One person, one identity

Admin

If the face being saved is already enrolled for a different student, saving is refused and the existing record is named. This blocks someone enrolling their face under a second matric number.

5.5  Correcting details & resetting a password

Admin /students/[id]
  1. Open the student and press Edit details.
  2. Correct the matric number or name, or pick new values from the gender, faculty, department, level and year dropdowns.
  3. To help a student who forgot their password, type a new one under Reset password.
  4. Untick Active to stop a student being recognised (for example after withdrawal) without deleting their history. Press Save changes.

Re-enrol face on a verified student repeats section 5.3 (identity check included) and replaces the stored templates.

5.6  Courses

Admin /courses
  1. Press + New course.
  2. Course code: choose the department prefix (e.g. CSC) and type the three-digit number. The department fills itself in.
  3. Type the title; pick level, semester, credit units, lecturer and required attendance from the dropdowns.
  4. Press Create course. Students can now register it from their portal.

5.7  Editing a course & assigning its lecturer

Admin /courses/[id]

Open a course and press Edit course. Pick a different Lecturer to hand the course over (or Unassigned), change any detail, and Save changes. Only the assigned lecturer can see the course and take its attendance.

5.8  Class lists

Admin /courses/[id]

Students normally register courses themselves. The administrator can still adjust a class list: + Add students opens a picker of students not yet on the course; Remove takes a student off. Only students on a course's class list are recognised in its lectures.

5.9  Staff accounts

Admin /staff

Choose a title (Prof., Dr., Engr., Mr., Mrs., Ms.), type the name, email and a password of at least six characters, pick the role and department, and press Create account. Create a lecturer here before assigning them a course.

5.10  Session monitor & reports

Admin /sessions · /reports

The session monitor lists every lecture across the department — course, lecturer, time, venue and how many were present. It is read-only: View register opens any register, but starting, capturing and closing belong to the lecturer. Attendance reports shows any course as a grid (section 6.6).

6. The lecturer portal

Running a lecture and taking attendance — for the courses assigned to you.

Sidebar: Dashboard · My courses · My lecture sessions · Attendance reports Sign in on the Lecturer tab.

6.1  Lecturer dashboard

Lecturer /dashboard

Your courses, sessions held, any class in progress (with Open camera), attendance marked today and recent records. Start a lecture session is at the top right.

6.2  My courses

Lecturer /courses

The courses assigned to you. Open one to see its class list (read-only) and session history, or to Start lecture session. If the list is empty, ask the administrator to assign you as the course's lecturer.

6.3  Starting a lecture session

Lecturer /sessions
  1. Choose the course, the venue (LT1, LT2, laboratories…) and Mark late after (5–45 minutes) from the dropdowns.
  2. Press Start session & open camera. Only one session per course can be open at a time.

6.4  Live capture — taking attendance

Lecturer /sessions/[id]/capture
  1. Open the capture screen a minute before you need it; wait until Start capture is enabled.
  2. Press Start capture and allow the camera. A red SCANNING badge appears; a frame is analysed about every second.
  3. Point the camera at the students. Each face gets a coloured box and name label; Marked present fills up as they are recognised.
  4. For anyone the camera cannot pick up, press Mark manually beside their name.
  5. When the lecture ends, press Close session to lock the register.
Green — newly markedRecognised and just saved to the register.
Blue — already markedRecognised, already recorded this session. Nothing changes.
Red — not recognisedNot verified yet, or not registered on this course.
The status line under the video “N frames scanned · N faces seen · GPU” shows the system is working. If frames are not climbing, capture is paused. CPU mode (slower) means hardware acceleration is off (section 2.2).

6.5  Session register

Lecturer /sessions/[id]

Who was present, late and absent, with times and the method (face or manual — face records show the match distance). Export CSV downloads it; Remove deletes a wrong record; Close / Reopen session locks or unlocks it.

6.6  Attendance reports

Lecturer & Admin /reports

Pick a course to see every student against every lecture (P present, L late, A absent), each student's percentage and exam eligibility, and the summary cards. Export CSV for the records.

9. Troubleshooting

The problems you are most likely to meet, and what to do.

SymptomWhy it happensWhat to do
The camera button says Preparing…The face engine is loading and preparing (first time in this browser session).Wait 5–30 seconds; the panel shows progress. Next time, open the page earlier.
“Face engine: CPU mode”The browser's hardware acceleration is off.Capture still works, more slowly. Turn on Use graphics acceleration in browser settings and restart the browser.
“Complete the identity check above to unlock the camera”By design.Choose the document checked and tick the confirmation box.
Capture says “Looking for a face”No face in the frame, or it is very small.Have the student face the camera at about an arm's length. Follow the on-screen prompt.
“More than one face in view”Someone else is in the frame.Only the student being enrolled should face the camera.
“This face is already enrolled for …”The person is already enrolled under another matric number.Investigate before going further — this is the impersonation check working.
“Camera permission was denied”The browser blocked access.Click the padlock in the address bar, set Camera to Allow, reload.
Wrong-tab message at sign-inCorrect details on another portal's tab.Switch to the tab the message names.
“This record has no online account yet”The student was created before online accounts existed.Administrator: Edit details → Reset password.
Matric or JAMB number “already registered”Someone registered with it before.If it is not the student's own account, the administrator investigates and corrects.
A red box — a real student is not recognisedNot verified yet, not on this course's class list, or inactive.Mark manually now; then verify the student or check their courses.
Lecturer sees no coursesNo course is assigned to them.Administrator: open the course → Edit course → Lecturer.
“This session is closed”The lecture was closed.Reopen it from the register if it was closed by mistake.

Known limits of the prototype

  • Lighting at the extremes. Dim and washed-out frames are corrected automatically, but near-darkness or a face strongly backlit by a window can still be missed. Manual marking covers this.
  • Range. Faces must be reasonably large in the frame; one camera at the front will not reach the back of a 300-seat hall.
  • No liveness detection. A printed photograph held up to the camera could pass; defeating that needs blink or depth sensing, outside this prototype's scope.
  • The threshold is a trade-off. 0.5 is a sensible default, not a certainty.

10. Demonstration checklist & quick reference

Keep this page open on the day.

Before the panel arrives

  1. Use Chrome or Edge with hardware acceleration on, on a laptop with a working webcam, facing a light source.
  2. Sign in as Administrator and open Students → Verify & enrol for the student you will enrol. Wait until the button reads Start face capture and the line underneath says GPU-accelerated. The engine is now prepared for the whole session.
  3. In a second browser window (or after the enrolment), sign in as Lecturer and open a live capture screen once, so it is ready too.
  4. Have a passport photo ready on the laptop for the live registration.

The demonstration, in order

  1. Student: /register → show the dropdowns and the matric number building itself → Create my account → Course registration.
  2. Administrator: Students → Awaiting verification → Verify & enrol → check the JAMB slip → tick → Start face capture (about 2 seconds) → Verify student & save face.
  3. Impersonation check: try to enrol the same face for another student → refused.
  4. Lecturer: My lecture sessions → Start session → Start capture → green box → Close session.
  5. Student: sign back in → the lecture counts on the dashboard.
